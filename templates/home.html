How it works

From sign-up to certificate

Step 2

Join or build a course

Browse published courses, or create modules, lessons and downloadable resources of your own.

Step 3

Learn, submit and certify

Complete lessons, submit assignments, take quizzes, track progress and earn a verifiable certificate.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about accounts, courses and certificates.

Is Shikshya free to use?

Creating an account is free. Some courses are free and others are paid; the price is shown on each course card and detail page.

Who can create and publish courses?

Teachers can create courses after an administrator approves the account. Administrators review and publish courses before they appear in the public catalogue.

How are certificates issued and verified?

Certificates are issued after a learner meets the course completion requirements. Each certificate has a unique code that can be checked on the public verification page.

Does Shikshya work on mobile devices?

Yes. The interface is responsive and works in modern mobile browsers on Android and iPhone, including lessons, forms and dashboards.

Can a school or training centre use Shikshya?

Yes. The platform supports student, teacher and administrator roles plus course, assessment, attendance and reporting features. Contact us to discuss your institution's requirements.
